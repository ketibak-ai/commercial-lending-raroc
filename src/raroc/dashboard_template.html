Commercial Lending Portfolio: RAROC

Synthetic book of 1,000 term loans, 2,000 revolvers, 20 interest rate derivatives, 2,000 deposit accounts and 2,000 payments services across 500 client relationships. The focus is the 10 key relationships, where deposits and treasury management decide whether the credit earns its capital.

RAROC by product

Credit products carry nearly all the capital. Revolvers fall below the 12% hurdle on their own. Deposits and payments use little capital, so their stand-alone RAROC is very high.

Key relationships: lending vs. full relationship RAROC

Each row runs from credit-only RAROC to full relationship RAROC. The gap is the value of deposits and payments. Relationships under 15% go on the pricing watch list.

Deal pricing examples

The pricing engine solves for the spread that earns the hurdle. It gives a stand-alone floor, a relationship floor and a cost floor, and recommends the higher of the relationship and cost floors.

All data is synthetic and generated with a fixed seed; it represents no real institution or client. Source, methodology, API and AI pricing copilot on GitHub.
