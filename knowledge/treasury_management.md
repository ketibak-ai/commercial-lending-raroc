# Treasury Management and Payments

## Services
The bank offers ACH origination, wire transfers, RTP instant payments, lockbox, positive pay fraud protection, account reconciliation, remote deposit capture and commercial card. Fees are charged per item, except commercial card which earns interchange of approximately 1.25% of spend.

## Profitability
Payments revenue is annual gross fees less earnings credit (ECR) offsets. The cost-to-income ratio for payments is 55%. Fraud and operational losses are budgeted at 0.5% of gross fees. Payments carry only operational risk capital (15% of revenue), so their standalone RAROC is very high.

## Role in Relationship Pricing
Treasury management is the primary driver of operating deposits and client stickiness. A client using payments services and holding operating accounts is significantly less likely to move its credit facilities. Payment volumes and fees are key inputs to relationship RAROC and support credit pricing concessions.
