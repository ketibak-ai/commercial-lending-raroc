# Deposit Funds Transfer Pricing Policy

## FTP Credit for Deposits
Deposits receive a funds transfer pricing (FTP) credit reflecting their value as funding. The credit is the matched-duration FTP rate on the stable portion of the balance, plus 85% of the 3-month FTP rate on the volatile portion. Deposit net interest income = balance x (FTP credit - rate paid).

## Deposit Types and Runoff
Operating deposits (analyzed DDA accounts used for payments and payroll) are assumed to have a 3-year behavioral duration and a 25% runoff haircut, consistent with LCR operating deposit treatment. Non-operating deposits (money market and sweep) carry a 0.5-year duration and a 40% runoff haircut. Commercial CDs use contractual maturity and a 10% haircut.

## Deposit Pricing Guidance
Operating deposits are the most valuable funding source because they are sticky and low cost. Relationship managers should prioritise operating balances linked to treasury management services. Non-operating balances paying rates close to the FTP curve add little value and should not be used to justify loan pricing concessions.

## Earnings Credit Rate
Analyzed commercial accounts may receive an earnings credit rate (ECR) that offsets treasury management fees. ECR offsets reduce reported fee income and should be considered together with the deposit spread when evaluating relationship profitability.
