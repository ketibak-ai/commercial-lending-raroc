# Interest Rate Derivatives Policy

## Permitted Products
Client interest rate derivatives are offered only to hedge borrowing by existing credit clients. Permitted products are pay-fixed interest rate swaps, interest rate caps and collars. Speculative trading for clients is not permitted.

## Market Risk
All client trades are hedged back-to-back with dealer counterparties, so the bank retains no material market risk. Revenue is the sales credit (bps running on notional) embedded in the client rate.

## Counterparty Credit Risk
Derivatives create counterparty exposure measured with a simplified SA-CCR approach: EAD = 1.4 x (positive MTM + add-on x notional). Derivative exposure is included in the borrower's total credit limit and shares the borrower's risk rating. A 25% CVA capital add-on applies. LGD for derivative exposure is 40%.

## Relationship Value
Hedging clients typically have larger floating-rate term loans. Derivative sales credit improves relationship RAROC but also consumes credit capital, so the net contribution must be evaluated at relationship level.
