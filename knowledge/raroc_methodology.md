# RAROC Methodology

## Definition
Risk-adjusted return on capital (RAROC) measures after-tax, risk-adjusted income per dollar of economic capital. RAROC = (Revenue - Operating Expense - Expected Loss + Capital Credit) x (1 - Tax Rate) / Economic Capital. The tax rate is 24%. Capital credit is the funding benefit of equity, credited at the 1-year FTP rate of 3.85%.

## Hurdle Rate
The enterprise hurdle rate is 12%, representing the cost of equity. A deal or relationship with RAROC at or above 12% creates shareholder value. Economic value added (EVA) = Net Income - 12% x Economic Capital. Positive EVA means the business clears the hurdle.

## Expected Loss
Expected loss (EL) = PD x LGD x EAD. PD comes from the internal 1-10 risk rating master scale (rating 1 = 0.03%, rating 5 = 0.50%, rating 7 = 1.60%, rating 10 = 15%). LGD depends on collateral: Senior Secured 30%, Real Estate 35%, Unsecured 45%. Expected loss is a cost of doing business and is charged against revenue.

## Economic Capital
Credit capital uses the Basel IRB corporate formula at 99.9% confidence with maturity adjustment (maturity floored at 1 year and capped at 5 years), scaled by an economic-capital multiplier of 1.06. Operational risk capital is 15% of revenue (basic indicator approach). Deposits carry operational and ALM capital of 0.40% of balances. Unexpected loss, not expected loss, drives capital.

## Exposure at Default
Term loans: EAD equals the outstanding balance. Revolvers: EAD = drawn balance + 75% credit conversion factor x undrawn commitment. Interest rate derivatives: simplified SA-CCR, EAD = 1.4 x (positive mark-to-market + add-on x notional), with add-ons of 0.5% (up to 1 year), 1.5% (1-5 years) and 3.0% (over 5 years). A 25% CVA capital add-on applies to derivative credit capital.

## Relationship RAROC
Relationship RAROC aggregates net income and economic capital across every account a client holds: term loans, revolvers, derivatives, deposits and payments. Lending RAROC includes only credit products (loans, revolvers, derivatives). Ancillary uplift = Relationship RAROC - Lending RAROC and quantifies the value of deposits and treasury management cross-sell.
